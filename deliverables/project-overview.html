Cosmetic Product Advisor
Project overview · 6 screens

Local-first decision support

Skincare choices,
with evidence.

A private advisor for comparing sunscreen and moisturiser in India. It connects sourced product facts to clear rules, then explains what the evidence can—and cannot—support.

Current: searchable 10-product catalogRecommendations and agentic workflow are planned.

01 / Problem

Choosing a product is harder than finding one

Shoppers must reconcile claims, ingredients, prices and reviews across scattered sources.

01 / FRAGMENTED

Facts live everywhere

Price, pack, ingredient declaration and source dates are rarely compared in one place.

02 / PERSONAL

Fit needs context

A budget, skin type and ingredients to avoid can change which products qualify.

03 / UNCERTAIN

Reviews need limits

Small or mixed samples should not become confident product promises.

Project responseShow provenance, apply deterministic eligibility rules, and surface uncertainty alongside every shortlist.
